Parties and conclusion

Controller is the school or its legal entity (school authority) for which the teacher uses EduGrade. If a teacher uses EduGrade without their school's approval, they are the controller themselves within the meaning of Art. 4(7) GDPR.

Processor: Fabian Murauer, avocloud.net, Rablstraße 23a/4.4, 4600 Wels, Austria – fabian.murauer@avocloud.net

The agreement is concluded electronically (Art. 28(9) GDPR): it is signed in a separate step before first using EduGrade, with the name of the signing person (the controller or their representative, see below). Name, school (if any), time and contract version are stored in the account. A material change to the agreement requires signing again.

Conclusion by the school: If a school uses EduGrade as an organisation, the school management or a person authorised by it signs this agreement for the school. It applies to all teachers who belong to the organisation. Schools that need a separately signed agreement can request one by email at fabian.murauer@avocloud.net.

Conclusion by the teacher with approval of the school management: The teacher concludes this agreement on behalf of the school and names the school management. Until the school management confirms the conclusion, the agreement applies with the teacher personally. Upon confirmation, the school takes the teacher's place in this agreement.

Conclusion by the teacher on their own responsibility: If a teacher uses EduGrade without their school's approval, this agreement applies with them personally.

1 Subject matter and duration

The subject matter of this agreement is the provision of the web-based grade management system EduGrade (storage, synchronisation and retrieval of the data entered by the controller) by the processor.

The agreement applies for as long as the controller uses EduGrade; where the school concludes it, for as long as the organisation or the teacher's account exists. It ends automatically when the account or organisation is deleted.

2 Nature and purpose of processing

The processor stores the controller's data in encrypted form and makes it available for retrieval and editing. The sole purpose is the keeping of grades, assessments, attendance and participation records by the teacher.

No processing takes place for the processor's own purposes (e.g. analysis, advertising, disclosure).

3 Types of data and categories of data subjects

Data subjects: students and teachers recorded in EduGrade by the controller.

Types of data:

  • Student names, class and subject assignment
  • Grades, assessments and related notes
  • Attendance, participation and behaviour notes
  • When using an organisation: student name, class and teacher name in the organisation-wide student list
  • When using the WebUntis or Moodle integration: credentials and the class, course and timetable data retrieved with them

The controller ensures that no special categories of personal data (Art. 9 GDPR) are recorded, in particular no health information, diagnoses, information on therapies or religious belief in notes, comments, attendance remarks or the participation log.

4 Instructions

The processor processes the data only on documented instructions from the controller. Using the functions of EduGrade (entering, changing, exporting, sharing, deleting) in particular counts as an instruction. If the processor considers an instruction to be unlawful, it informs the controller without delay.

Processing without instruction only takes place where the processor is legally obliged to do so; in that case it informs the controller beforehand unless the law prohibits this.

7 Deletion and return

The controller can retrieve their data at any time using the export function (return). When the account is deleted, all contract data is deleted without delay. It remains in backup copies until regular overwriting, at the latest after 30 days. The record of conclusion of this agreement (name, school, time, contract version) is kept for three years after the agreement ends. It contains no student data.

Accounts that have not been used for twelve months are deleted by the processor after three announcements by email (30, 7 and 1 day beforehand). The controller hereby already gives the instruction to do so.

Annex 1 – Technical and organisational measures (Art. 32 GDPR)

EncryptionAll gradebook data is stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from the teacher's password (PBKDF2) and is not stored permanently on the server; it is only available in memory during an active session. Without the teacher's password or recovery key the data cannot be decrypted. The server sees the password at sign-in and keeps the key in memory during the session; without an active session the data cannot be decrypted by the operator either.
ExceptionsNot encrypted with the teacher's key are: (1) class shares for students/parents and class handovers to other teachers: these are encrypted with a server key so recipients can open them without the teacher's password. The share entry additionally contains student names, class and teacher name unencrypted; PINs are stored only as hashes. The server key for shares is located on the server. Shares can therefore technically be decrypted by the operator. The snapshot also includes notes and participation (not the behaviour log). Handovers are deleted on acceptance, decline or expiry; (2) when using an organisation, student name, class and teacher name for the organisation-wide student list (no grades); (3) the account's school name.
Physical access controlThe servers are located in the STRATO GmbH data centre in Germany. Physical access control, fire protection and power supply are the responsibility of STRATO; evidence of its ISO/IEC 27001 certification.
TransportHTTPS/TLS only. Emails are sent via our own mail server using TLS (including certificate verification).
AuthenticationPasswords as salted PBKDF2 hashes; optional passwordless sign-in via a paired smartphone; sessions with secure httpOnly cookies and limited lifetime; rate limiting against brute-force attacks.
Access controlEach teacher can only access their own data. Only the operator has administrative server access; SSH access is key-based only (no password login). The customer account with the hosting provider is protected by a unique password and two-factor authentication.
Application securityCSRF protection, Content Security Policy, input validation, regular updates of the server software.
ReviewThe measures are reviewed at least annually and upon material changes. Dependencies are continuously checked for known security vulnerabilities.
Incident handlingOperation is monitored (health check of the service). Security incidents are documented, assessed and reported in accordance with section 5.
AvailabilityHosting in a data centre in Germany. Daily database backup, encrypted with AES-256-GCM (key kept separately from the server), retained for 30 days, stored on the server in Germany. Restoration was successfully tested on 2026-10-02 and is tested at least annually. In addition, an export function for the teacher's own backups.
Data minimisationNo analytics, tracking or advertising services. Server logs with IP addresses are kept for at most 30 days.
DeletionDeleting the account removes all contract data immediately. Backups are overwritten after 30 days at the latest.
