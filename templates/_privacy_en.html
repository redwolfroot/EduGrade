2 Two roles: account data and student data

Account data (e.g. your email address) is processed by us as controller; this privacy policy applies to it.

Student data (names, grades, attendance etc.) is entered by you as a teacher. You or your school are the controller for it, and we act as your processor under Art. 28 GDPR. The details are set out in the Data Processing Agreement (DPA), which you sign before first use.

As controller for student data you need a legal basis – at schools this is usually the statutory duty to assess performance (Art. 6(1)(c) or (e) GDPR); parental consent is normally not required. Please agree the use of EduGrade with your school management and inform parents and students as part of your school's data protection notice. EduGrade can create a template for this as a PDF.

3 What data we process

Account data

  • Username, email address and whether it is confirmed
  • Password (only as a PBKDF2 hash), hash of your recovery key
  • Name of your school – stored unencrypted so that other teachers are offered known school names while typing (only the school name, not who entered it)
  • Signature of the DPA: name, school, time, contract version
  • Paired smartphone: device name, time of pairing and last use, and a hash of the device key
  • Security data: number of failed sign-ins and any account locks
  • Membership of an organisation (school) and your role in it

Gradebook (encrypted)

  • Classes, school years, subjects, categories and settings
  • Student names, grades, assessments, attendance, participation, behaviour notes and notes
  • Credentials for WebUntis and Moodle, if you connect them

Technical data

  • Sessions: a random session key, creation and expiry time
  • IP address: in memory for rate limiting (protection against attacks) and for sign-in attempts; together with browser and operating system it is shown on your paired smartphone so you can spot unknown sign-in attempts
  • Server logs with IP addresses for security purposes; email addresses appear there only in shortened form
  • Sign-in code emails state the IP address and browser of the attempt; before sign-in the name of the paired phone is shown so you enter the right code
  • Within an organisation, confirmed members can search other members by email address and username when handing over a class

5 Storage location and encryption

All data is stored on servers of STRATO GmbH in data centres in Germany. Emails are sent from a mail server we operate ourselves. Your account and gradebook data is not transferred to countries outside the EU (for the DNS service see the recipients section; for external links see section 7).

Your gradebook is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your password when you sign in (PBKDF2) and is kept only in the server's memory during an active session – never permanently. Without your password, your recovery key or your paired smartphone the stored data cannot be decrypted. When you sign in, the server sees your password and keeps the key in memory until the session ends; without an active session the data cannot be decrypted by us. Encryption and decryption happen on the server, not in the browser; true end-to-end encryption is planned.

Exceptions – this data is not encrypted with your personal key:

  • Class shares and class handovers (section 6): encrypted with a server key so that students or the receiving teacher can open them without your password
  • Organisation student list: student name, class and teacher name (no grades)
  • Your account's school name

Further measures: HTTPS for every connection, sign-in with a confirmation code by email or smartphone, account lock after repeated failed attempts, CSRF protection, Content Security Policy, server access only for the operator via SSH key. Details are in annex 1 of the DPA.

6 Features with additional processing

Class sharing (student access)

You can give students or parents access to their grades via a link and a personal PIN. For this, a snapshot of the class (students, grades, participation, notes, categories, subjects – without the behaviour log) is stored encrypted with a server key and updated when you make changes. After entering the PIN, each person sees only their own data, to the extent you choose. The share expires after the chosen period and is deleted shortly afterwards; you can revoke it at any time.

Organisations

If your school joins as an organisation, all confirmed members see a shared student list with student name, class and teacher (no grades). The organisation administrators also see the members' usernames and email addresses.

Class handover

If you hand a class over to another teacher in your organisation, a copy (to the extent you choose) is stored encrypted with a server key until it is accepted or declined, for at most 7 days; after that it is deleted.

WebUntis and Moodle

If you connect WebUntis or Moodle, your credentials are stored encrypted in your gradebook. The server connects on your behalf to your school's WebUntis or Moodle server to fetch class lists and timetables or to add events. Your school or its provider is responsible for the processing there.

Smartphone app and pairing

When you pair a smartphone, it creates a secret key that stays in the device's protected storage. The server only holds a hash of it and your data key encrypted with it. The app stays signed in for up to 6 months.

Recovery key

When you register you receive a recovery key that lets you reset your password without losing data. On request we send it to you once as a PDF by email; we keep no readable copy afterwards. If you reset your password by email without the recovery key, your gradebook is deleted because it cannot be decrypted without the key.

7 External content

EduGrade does not load scripts, fonts or stylesheets from third parties; all libraries and fonts are served from our own server. No IP address is therefore sent to third parties when you open the app.

The only exception are links: if you follow the link to the donation page Ko-fi or other external links, that page opens in a new tab and the provider receives your IP address. Nothing is embedded.

8 Cookies and local storage

  • edugrade_domain_move (essential cookie): remembers that you dismissed the notice about the domain move
  • session_token (essential cookie, httpOnly): keeps you signed in – 1 hour in the browser, up to 6 months in the app
  • Your browser's local storage: settings such as language, colour scheme and compact view; they never leave your device
  • Service worker: caches program files so EduGrade loads faster

There are no tracking or advertising cookies, so no cookie banner is needed.

10 Retention

  • Account and gradebook: as long as your account exists; all data is deleted immediately when the account is deleted, including shares, class handovers, your organisation entries and open reset links
  • Unconfirmed accounts: released and deleted after 24 hours if the email address is registered again
  • Inactive accounts: may be deleted after 12 months of inactivity with prior notice
  • Sessions: until expiry (1 hour, or 6 months in the app) or sign-out
  • Class shares: until expiry or revocation, then deleted within one day
  • Class handovers: until accepted or declined, at most 7 days; the copy is deleted on acceptance, decline or expiry
  • Server logs: at most 30 days

11 Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21 GDPR). Much of this you can do directly in EduGrade: edit data, export it as a file or delete your account with all data. For anything else, write to fabian.murauer@avocloud.net.

Students and parents with requests about student data should contact the teacher or school as controller; we support them in this.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.
